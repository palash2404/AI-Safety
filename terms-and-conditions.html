Terms & Conditions

These Terms and Conditions (“T&Cs”) govern how AI Safety Saarland (“we”, “us”, “our”) delivers free educational activities, community events, and career advice services to individuals (“you”, “participants”) who engage with our work. These T&Cs should be read alongside our Privacy Notice and must be accepted before you take part in any of our services.

1. About Us

AI Safety Saarland is a nonprofit based in Germany focused on reducing global catastrophic risks from advanced AI. We coordinate events, courses, discussion groups, and community infrastructure to support participants’ personal development and career progression in high-impact fields.

All services are offered free of charge. Participation is voluntary. We do not provide services to individuals under the age of 18.

2. Scope of Services

Depending on your engagement, we may provide access to:

  • Educational courses, research projects, group discussions, and reading groups
  • In-person or virtual events, meetups, hackathons, or retreats
  • Online community platforms (e.g., Slack, Airtable)
  • Light-touch career advice or referrals to opportunities in AI safety or effective altruism (EA)-aligned fields

Participation may require submitting a form, attending an interview, or sharing your availability. Acceptance into structured programs is selective and based on alignment with program goals and available capacity.

3. What We Expect From Participants

By taking part in our activities, you agree to:

  • Submit accurate information in your forms and communications
  • Engage meaningfully and respectfully in sessions you are accepted into
  • Abide by community standards and behave professionally on all platforms (e.g., Slack, Zoom)
  • Notify us if your availability changes or if you can no longer participate

We reserve the right to pause or withdraw your access in cases of:

  • Disrespectful or harmful conduct
  • Inactivity that affects the experience of others
  • Misuse of internal platforms or breaches of confidentiality

4. Data Handling & Communications

We handle personal data in line with our Privacy Notice. This includes data submitted in application forms, participation logs, Slack activity, and career conversations.

By participating, you agree that:

  • Your data may be used for program coordination, evaluation, and improvement
  • We may contact you with updates related to your involvement
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications (e.g., newsletters) at any time
  • If you receive career support, notes may be taken, with your awareness, to personalise our advice

5. Referrals & Career Support

Where you request or consent to career guidance:

  • We may share your profile (e.g., background, interests, goals) with relevant EA and AI safety organisations
  • This only happens with your informed consent
  • We do not guarantee outcomes such as internships, job offers, or funding
  • All referrals are made with the intent of supporting your path into high-impact work

You can withdraw from referral pathways at any time by contacting us.

6. Program Discretion

We strive to deliver our services reliably, but due to limited capacity and evolving priorities, we may:

  • Decline or defer applications
  • Change program timelines or content
  • Replace facilitators or restructure discussion groups
  • Pause or cancel underperforming or under-resourced initiatives

We aim to communicate major changes in advance where feasible, but reserve the right to act quickly when necessary.

7. No Guarantee of Outcomes

Our programs are informational and community-based. Participation does not guarantee:

  • Admission to any specific career pathway
  • Paid opportunities or funding
  • Endorsement by other EA or AI safety organisations

We are not responsible for decisions made by third parties (e.g., employers or grantmakers to whom you are referred).

8. Governing Law

These T&Cs are governed by the laws of Germany. In the event of a dispute, we encourage participants to first contact us directly at info@ais-saarland.org to seek an informal resolution. If this is not successful, disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Saarbrücken, Germany.
