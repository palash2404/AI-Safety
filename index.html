About AI Safety Saarland

Students from Saarland University working to make AI go well.

We are a student-led initiative passionate about driving change. Through reading groups, fellowships and events, we bring people from every discipline together to understand the risks from advanced AI — and to work on reducing them.

Why it matters

Why AI safety is important.

AI systems are getting more capable every year and are being built into more of the world. Making sure they stay safe, controllable and beneficial is one of the defining challenges of our generation.

01

Capabilities are moving fast

Each new generation of models can do more, and they're being deployed into research, business and public life before we fully understand them.

02

Safety is still an open problem

Reliably getting advanced systems to do what we intend — and understanding what happens inside them — are research questions we haven't solved yet.

03

The stakes are shared

Mistakes or misuse could affect everyone. That's why technical research, good governance and informed public debate matter now, not later.

Our mission

To foster interdisciplinary exchange, education, and research addressing large-scale risks from AI development and deployment at Saarland University and beyond.

Programs

Two ways to go deeper into AI safety.

Start with the fundamentals, or join the advanced track and work on the problems that matter most.

Introductory Fellowship

Weekly

Learn the fundamentals of AI Safety and Governance through a weekly reading and discussion group.

  • Explore the key challenges: technical safety, governance, power dynamics, and societal impact
  • Discuss current approaches to ensuring AI systems remain controllable and beneficial
  • Build understanding in a supportive, interdisciplinary community
Find out more →

Advanced Fellowship

Hands-on

Go deeper into the technical methods shaping the future of AI safety through focused reading, discussion, and practice.

  • Evaluation: how we test what capabilities AI models have and how they act in certain situations
  • Interpretability: what we can learn about AI models by looking at their internal workings
  • Engage with active research: discuss key papers, run experiments, and hear from researchers
Find out more →
